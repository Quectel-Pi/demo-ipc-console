# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than individuals, and accept constructive feedback.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing another person's private information, sustained disruption, and sexualized or insulting conduct.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review discussions, community forums used for project collaboration, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an available private repository or profile contact channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and impartially as reasonably possible.
